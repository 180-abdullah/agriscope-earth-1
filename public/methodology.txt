AGRISCOPE EARTH — METHODOLOGY ASE-0.2

AgriScope Earth is a research-screening system, not an official warning,
diagnosis, audited carbon footprint or replacement for field measurements.

Six missions:
1. Flood and crop exposure
2. Crop stress
3. Wetland and land-use change
4. Irrigation water and energy
5. Agricultural greenhouse gases
6. Fire and heat

Every result declares whether its evidence is observed, near-real-time,
forecast, modelled, calculated, user-supplied, demonstration or unavailable.

Full equations, assumptions, source links and limitations are available in
docs/METHODOLOGY.md, docs/DATA_SOURCES.md and
docs/SCIENTIFIC_VALIDATION.md in the repository.
